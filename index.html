An island that is yours

Every character wakes on a small island in the sky. Clear it, wake the gathering nodes sleeping under the grass, and buy new land beside it piece by piece.

Gather and craft

Trees, ore, crops and fishing spots grow back after you take from them. What you gather becomes tools, weapons and armor at the stations, rolled fresh every time.

Fight your way up

Swords, scythes, gauntlets, staffs and summoning books each fight their own way. Slot mods into your gear, master a weapon, and take on what guards the rarest drops.

A wiki written by players
